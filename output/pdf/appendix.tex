statement here was checked against the named source files. It omits the deleted

\subsection{Current settings and a deliberate trade}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{.30\textwidth}X}
\toprule
Setting or behavior & Current source-checked meaning \\
\midrule
Automatic plane reuse & After a collision-free trial changes the retained safe duration by no more than the arrival-time tolerance and the same path--region pairs remain involved, the engine reuses its separating planes on the next trajectory calculation. This continuation rule is not a planner option. \\
Reuse tolerance & The existing \texttt{ArrivalTimeTolerance\_s} owns both convergence and the automatic reuse boundary. \\
Repeat limit & The BMTP loop has at most 35 iterations. It can stop when the safe arrival improvement reaches the arrival-time tolerance or a time/work limit is reached. There is no stagnation-stop setting or diagnostic in current source. \\
Removed settings & \texttt{EnablePlaneReuse}, \texttt{PlaneReuseImprovementTolerance\_s}, \texttt{CollocationSegmentCount}, \texttt{MaximumCollocationSegmentCount}, \texttt{MaximumMeshRefinementPasses}, \texttt{MaximumNlpFunctionEvaluations}, \texttt{OptimalityTolerance}, and \texttt{StepTolerance} are not planner options in the current resolver. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Changes that made the old material wrong}

The old engine-design document is wrong where it describes two former
warm-start helper functions. Neither exists in the current source tree. It is
also wrong where it says the stagnation stop is part of the engine, and where
it says separating-plane reuse is off by default. Current source sets that
option to true. These are corrections, not editorial choices.

\subsection{Limits of the documentation record}

The engine records trial durations, collision-pair counts, collision-free flags,
and reuse diagnostics. It does not retain every intermediate trial curve. That
is why Figure~\ref{fig:repeats} plainly labels its three curve shapes as
explanatory sketches, while the times and statuses are exported data. I did not
infer missing intermediate geometry.
